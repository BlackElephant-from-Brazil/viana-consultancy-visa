Before you get in touch

You do not need to have everything worked out.

Do I need to know whether I should apply for D7 or D8?

No. Tell us about your income, work and plans. We can explain the next step for assessing which route may be appropriate. Eligibility requires an individual assessment.

Can you help if I want to prepare the documents myself?

Yes. We offer document review and legal guidance as well as broader assistance. The scope depends on your circumstances and the support you need.

Can I get in touch about moving with my partner or children?

Yes. Mention who would be moving with you and any timing considerations. Family circumstances are an important part of planning the appropriate approach.

Can you help beyond the visa application?

Our assistance can include the subsequent residence-permit stage in Portugal. Your proposal will specify which stages and services are included.

What if I already have an application underway?

Briefly describe the type of application, its current stage and the issue you want help with. If there is an official notice or deadline, mention it so we can understand the context and advise on the appropriate next step.

Can you guarantee approval or a moving date?

No. Decisions and appointment availability remain with the relevant authorities, and banking procedures are subject to the bank's assessment. We can help prepare the agreed parts of your case and explain the dependencies.

What if I am only exploring a future move?

You can still get in touch. Let us know your intended timing and the questions you want to resolve. Planning may begin well before it is appropriate to submit an application.

Does sending the form start a legal service?

No. The form is an initial enquiry. It does not book a consultation or create an attorney-client relationship. Any consultation or further service will be agreed separately.
